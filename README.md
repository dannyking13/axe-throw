# Axe Throw

## About the game
Axe Throw turns a lumberjack's pastime into a razor-sharp game of nerve. Line
up your throw, read the moving target, and let the axe fly — the deep *thunk*
of a clean hit is instantly addictive. Just when you find your rhythm, the game
throws in explosive bombs orbiting the target: clip one and your perfect streak
goes up in smoke. Every throw is a tiny duel between patience and greed.

Quick rounds, escalating target patterns and the constant temptation to shoot
just one more axe make it dangerously easy to lose track of time. Simple to
learn in seconds, hard to put down for hours.

## Controls

### Smartphone & tablet
- **Tap** and hold to aim.
- **Release** your finger to hurl the axe at the target.

### Desktop
- **Left-click** and hold to aim.
- **Release** the button to throw the axe.
> Trackpad: single click = same as left-click.
